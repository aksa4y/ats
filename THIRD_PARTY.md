Source: https://github.com/shortcord/modguard-linux-unoffical
Commit: 7ae63b632ab56349b5f538b3aa9f3da1d66be3dd

The files in vendor/modguard and tests/exhaust_model_test.cpp are unchanged excerpts from this source.
The upstream README states that its Linux implementation has no general source license.
This project does not relicense those files or claim permission from their author.
No blanket open-source license applies to this repository.
The upstream SCS SDK license applies only to its SDK definitions, not to the exhaust model.

The original MODGuard.dll, smoke archives, textures, and truck meshes are not included.
The installer downloads the unchanged companion archive from that upstream revision.
The archive remains subject to its original rights. It is not published in our release assets.
